Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our events

Registering for events is simple and easy:

  1. Go to the Registration Page
  2. Fill in your personal details (Name, Roll Number, Branch, Email, Phone)
  3. Select the events you want to participate in
  4. Agree to the terms and conditions
  5. Click "Submit Registration"
  6. You'll receive a confirmation email with your registration details

Note: You can register for multiple events at once.

Most of our events are completely free for all students. However:

  • Free Events: Technical Quiz, Coding Contest, Workshop, Poster Presentation, Gallery viewing
  • Paid Events: Some premium workshops and cultural events may have a nominal fee (₹50-200)
  • Hackathon: Completely free, but limited seats (first come, first served)

Fee information is clearly mentioned on each event's details page.

Yes, absolutely! You can participate in as many events as you want. However, please note:

  • Some events may have conflicting timings - check the schedule carefully
  • Make sure you don't miss registration deadlines for any event
  • Some events have limited seating capacity - register early
  • You can register for multiple events in a single registration form

Pro Tip: Visit our Schedule Page to avoid timing conflicts.

After successful registration, you'll receive:

  1. Confirmation Email: Sent immediately to your registered email address
  2. Registration ID: A unique ID for tracking your registration
  3. Event Details: Date, time, venue, and any special instructions
  4. Reminder Email: 24 hours before the event
Note: If you don't receive the confirmation email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact us at events@college.edu

Yes, you can modify your registration:

  • Before Event: You can cancel or modify registration until 24 hours before the event
  • Cancellation Process: Contact the event coordinator or email events@college.edu with your registration ID
  • After Event: No cancellations allowed after the event has started
  • Refunds: If applicable, refunds will be processed within 7 business days

For quick cancellation, visit the Contact page and reach out to the event coordinator directly.
